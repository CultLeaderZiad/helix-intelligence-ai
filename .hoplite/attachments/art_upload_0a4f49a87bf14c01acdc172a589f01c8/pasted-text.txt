A. Discover reliability (P0)
Cancel and the UI timeout must cancel the server job, not just the screen. Add a cancel endpoint/state transition, make the worker stop the provider call, and record a terminal state.
Give every job a deadline, a worker heartbeat with lease renewal, and reclaim of expired leases. Every provider call gets a timeout. Prove that no job runs twice after a lease expires (idempotent completion).
Every job ends in exactly one terminal outcome: succeeded | failed(reason) | cancelled | timed_out. The UI shows it clearly in EN and AR with a retry action, and nothing gets stuck on "resolving sources".
Progress labels must come from real server stage events, not a fixed animation. Health labels must not claim provider success without evidence.
Reduce polling: back off, stop on a terminal state or a hidden tab, and drop duplicate requests.
Tests for: cancel mid-run, provider timeout, worker crash then reclaim, deadline exceeded, and duplicate-delivery idempotency. Run them on a disposable Postgres.
B. Narrow the product
Remove the "Trial ended — select a plan" banner and all trial/plan gating copy. Pending users see an honest "access pending admin approval" state, and approved users get access. Hide or remove "Billing & Meter".
Navigation shows only what is built. Discover stays primary. Loops and pages that are mock or unbuilt (per the audit) are hidden behind a feature flag, or shown as clearly labeled "coming soon" with no fake data. Remove dead and duplicated routes, and fix the unclear ones the audit lists.
Legal: make the footer and promises match reality. Remove encryption or deletion claims that aren't proven, or implement them. Either move sensitive data out of localStorage or disclose it honestly.
C. Onboarding and tour guide
The onboarding modal ("Step 1 of 3 • Zero Cost" industry picker) and the product tour (driver.js) are buggy.

Audit every step of onboarding and the tour in EN and AR (RTL), at desktop and mobile sizes. Fix:

the modal re-opening on every navigation

Remove the "Zero Cost" copy unless it's literally true. Tour steps may only reference features that are visible after part B.
Add a way to restart the tour from Settings or Help.
