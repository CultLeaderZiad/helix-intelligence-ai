# Quick Start: Running Your First Search

Discover is the entry point for uncovering competitor creatives, angles, and ad spending across the digital ecosystem.

---

## 1. Navigating to Discover

Click on **Discover** in the left sidebar navigation, or visit [/discover](/discover).

You will see the central query command bar with filter chips and search controls.

---

## 2. Choosing Your Query

You can search Discover in three ways:

1. **Brand or Competitor Name**:
   - Example: `Gymshark`, `Hims`, `Athletic Greens`, `Ridge Wallet`
   - *Best for*: Auditing an exact competitor's active catalog and recent angle pivots.
2. **Niche or Product Category**:
   - Example: `DTC skincare serum`, `AI presentation maker`, `b2b saas billing`
   - *Best for*: Exploring market-wide patterns, dominant formats, and winning hook archetypes.
3. **Target Persona or Problem Statement**:
   - Example: `busy founders productivity`, `knee joint pain relief`
   - *Best for*: Uncovering creative hooks speaking to specific pain points.

---

## 3. Selecting Filters & Language

Beneath the search bar, customize your scrape parameters:
- **Platform**: Filter by Meta (Facebook/Instagram), TikTok, or Google Ads.
- **Format**: Filter by **Video** (short-form UGC, motion ads) or **Static** (single image, carousel).
- **Sort By**: Choose **Composite Score (Highest First)**, **Durability (Days Active)**, or **Most Recent**.
- **Search Language**: Select between English, Spanish, Chinese, Dutch, or Arabic to target localized ad libraries.

---

## 4. Triggering the Search Loop

Press `Enter` or click the **Search** button.

1. **Real-time Pipeline Tracker**: Helix initializes an automated extraction job. You will see live progress indicators (`Targeting ad libraries` → `Extracting creatives` → `Analyzing hooks & scores`).
2. **Result Ingestion**: Results stream into your gallery table as soon as extraction completes (typically 3 to 8 seconds).
3. **Credit Deduction**: A standard discovery search costs **1.0 credit**.

> [!TIP]
> If a brand has run few or no ads recently, Helix automatically engages deep fallback providers to search broader indexes. If no results return, see [FAQ: Why Did My Search Return No Results?](/docs/user-guide/faq#why-did-my-search-return-no-results).
