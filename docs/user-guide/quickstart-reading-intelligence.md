# Quick Start: Reading the Intelligence Breakdown

Every creative discovered by Helix undergoes visual, copy, and performance analysis. Here is how to read the result cards and inspection drawer.

---

## 1. Opening a Creative

In your Discover search results or on the **Intelligence** tab:
1. Click on any creative card or table row.
2. An **Inspection Drawer** slides out from the right side of your screen.
3. The drawer displays high-resolution media previews, exact copy breakdown, performance duration, and algorithmic scores.

---

## 2. Understanding the Signal Scores

Helix calculates four specialized signal scores between 0 and 100 for each ad:

| Score | In-App Meaning | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hook Score** | *"How strongly the opening grabs attention in the first seconds."* | High hook score (>80) indicates the visual or textual pattern interrupt stops scrolling before users skip. |
| **Clarity Score** | *"How obvious the offer and message are at a glance."* | High clarity (>75) means viewers immediately understand what is sold, who it's for, and what action to take. |
| **Retention Score** | *"How likely people are to keep watching instead of scrolling away."* | Reflects pacing, dynamic cuts, and progressive disclosure that hold audience interest. |
| **Composite Score** | *"One combined 0–100 signal from the three scores above (not reported by Meta or ad platform)."* | A balanced index of overall creative strength. Ads with 80+ are classified as Tier A outliers. |

> [!NOTE]
> These scores are proprietary computational signals calculated by Helix AI models. They are **not** metrics reported by Meta, TikTok, or Google ad managers.

---

## 3. Reviewing Copy & Angle Analysis

Beneath the scoring bars, examine the structured creative anatomy:
- **Headline & Body**: The exact verbatim ad text pulled from the active ad library.
- **Call to Action (CTA)**: The primary conversion directive (e.g., *Shop Now*, *Get Started*, *Claim 20% Off*).
- **Core Angle / Narrative Hook**: The underlying psychological trigger identified in the script (e.g., *Social Proof*, *Us vs Them*, *Before/After Transformation*, *Founder Story*).
- **Language Selector**: If an ad was published in a foreign language (e.g. Spanish, Chinese, Arabic), use the top right language dragger to translate the copy directly into English.

---

## 4. Durability & Estimated Impressions

- **Days Active**: The duration this creative has remained live on the network without being shut off.
- **Evergreen Survivor**: An ad active for **14+ days** is marked as an *Evergreen Survivor*. Brands do not keep paying ad spend on unprofitable ads for weeks; surviving ads indicate proven conversions.
- **Impressions Estimate**: Displayed with an `(est)` badge when calculated from public audience reach and engagement indicators. See [Estimated vs. Real Data](/docs/user-guide/concept-estimated-vs-real-data) for our transparent data standards.
