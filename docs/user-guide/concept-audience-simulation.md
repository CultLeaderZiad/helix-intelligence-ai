# Core Concepts: Audience Simulation

Audience Simulation allows marketing teams to stress-test creative concepts, scripts, and angles against synthetic customer personas before launching paid campaigns.

---

## 1. What Audience Simulation IS

> **Official In-App Guidance:**
> *"An AI role-plays each selected persona reacting to this creative to catch objections, confusing claims, and compliance risk before you spend. This is a simulated rehearsal, not a prediction of real audience performance."*

Audience Simulation is a **qualitative creative rehearsal tool**. It acts like a digital focus group that never sleeps:
- **Catches Blind Spots**: Identifies confusing phrases, unaddressed objections, and weak offers before production.
- **Stress-Tests Angles**: Evaluates whether a skepticism-heavy persona will reject the opening claim or demand immediate proof.
- **Tone & Compliance Check**: Flags potentially hyperbolic claims that could violate ad-network policies.

---

## 2. What Audience Simulation IS NOT

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Helix deliberately enforces strict boundaries:**
> - **NOT a statistical performance predictor**: Simulation does not predict real click-through rates (CTR), conversion rates (CVR), return on ad spend (ROAS), or exact dollar revenue.
> - **NEVER mixed with real telemetry**: Every simulation output is visibly tagged as **Simulated / Rehearsal** and is never displayed alongside verified CTR or ad-spend analytics.
> - **NOT a replacement for real ad spend**: Real market validation comes only when real customers vote with their wallets and attention. Simulation exists to refine the creative *before* you spend that budget.

---

## 3. The Persona Repertoire

Helix models standard demographic and psychographic consumer segments:

| Persona Segment | Behavioral Characteristics | What It Stress-Tests |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Skeptical Value Buyer** | Price-sensitive, cynical of marketing claims, scrutinizes refund policies. | Does the ad justify its premium price point? Are proof points concrete? |
| **Impulsive Early Adopter** | Trend-driven, seeks novel solutions, quick to purchase if visually hooked. | Is the opening hook exciting enough? Is checkout friction minimal? |
| **Pragmatic Professional** | Busy, values efficiency, skeptical of buzzwords and fluff. | Does the copy get to the point within 3 seconds? |
| **Compliance & Trust Inspector** | Sensitive to safety, privacy, scientific claims, and fine print. | Are health or performance claims over-promising? |

---

## 4. How to Use Simulation Feedback

When you run a rehearsal in **Create Studio** ([/create](/create)):
1. Read the **Instant Objections** listed by the personas.
2. If two or more personas find the opening claim unbelievable, adjust your opening hook to lead with a demo or social proof.
3. If personas are confused about what product category you are selling, sharpen the headline in the second beat.
