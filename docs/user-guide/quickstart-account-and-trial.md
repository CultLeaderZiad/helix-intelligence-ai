# Quick Start: Account & 7-Day Trial

Get up and running with Helix Intelligence in under five minutes.

---

## 1. Creating Your Account

To get started, visit [/sign-up](/sign-up) and create your account:
- Enter your **Email address** and choose a secure **Password**.
- Or sign up with single click using Google Authentication if available.
- No credit card or upfront billing information is required to start testing.

When your account is created, your **7-Day Free Trial** begins immediately.

---

## 2. What the 7-Day Trial Includes

The trial is designed to give you unrestricted access to evaluate real competitor ad intelligence:

- **100 Trial Credits**: Automatically credited to your workspace upon signup.
- **Daily Discovery Queries**: Run up to 20 ad-library discovery searches every day.
- **Full Scoring Engine**: Instant access to Hook, Clarity, Retention, and Composite scores for every discovered creative.
- **Create Studio Credits**: Sufficient credits to test AI ad remixing, hook variant generation, and synthetic audience rehearsals.
- **Competitor Monitors**: Create automated monitor loops to track your top competitor brands.

> [!NOTE]
> Trial limits reset every night at **00:00 UTC**. You can monitor your daily quota and remaining credits at any time on the [Billing & Meter](/billing) page.

---

## 3. Account Status & Trial Banner

At the top of your workspace, you will always see an active status banner:
- **Remaining Trial Days**: Counts down from 7 days.
- **Active Credit Quota**: Shows credits used today versus your daily plan limit.
- **Upgrade Option**: When you are ready to transition your team to production limits, click **Upgrade Plan** in the navigation.
