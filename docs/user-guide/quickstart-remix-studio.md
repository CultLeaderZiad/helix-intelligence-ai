# Quick Start: Remix in Create Studio

Create Studio allows you to take high-performing competitor creative frameworks and generate original scripts, visual variants, and video briefs for your brand.

---

## 1. What "Remix in Create Studio" Does

When you click **Remix in Create Studio** on any creative in Discover or Swipe Files:
1. Helix extracts the **structural pattern** of the winning ad (opening hook mechanism, pacing, emotional premise, and CTA format).
2. It strips competitor branding, trademarks, and product claims.
3. It maps this winning structure into **Create Studio** ([/create](/create)), ready for you to input your brand details.

> [!NOTE]
> Remixing does *not* copy competitor content verbatim. It reverse-engineers the persuasive architecture and applies it to your product.

---

## 2. Setting Your Creative Brief

Inside Create Studio:
1. **Your Brand / Product**: Enter your brand name and primary product value proposition.
2. **Target Audience**: Select the customer profile you are addressing.
3. **Tone & Style**: Choose from *Direct-Response UGC*, *High-Production Editorial*, *Founder Explainer*, or *Problem/Agitate/Solve*.
4. **Hook Variations**: Request 3 to 5 distinct opening angles based on the remixed pattern.

---

## 3. Rehearsing with Synthetic Audiences

Before generating production media or spending advertising dollars:
1. Expand the **Simulate with Synthetic Audiences** section.
2. Select 2 to 4 target persona segments (e.g. *Skeptical Budget Buyer*, *Impulsive Early Adopter*, *Feature-Focused Technical Buyer*).
3. Click **Rehearse This Creative**.
4. The AI simulates immediate qualitative feedback: objections, confusing points, and emotional resonance.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Important Disclaimer**: Synthetic audience simulation is a *creative rehearsal tool*, not a statistical conversion predictor. It helps identify blind spots and objections before you produce media. See [Audience Simulation Concepts](/docs/user-guide/concept-audience-simulation) for full guidance.

---

## 4. Generating Production Media

When satisfied with your script and brief:
- **Generate Ad Image**: Click *Generate Image* (powered by Higgsfield AI models). Deducts **2.0 credits**.
- **Generate Ad Video**: Click *Generate Video Motion*. Deducts **5.0 credits**.
- Your generated assets appear in the output canvas and are saved to your workspace library.
