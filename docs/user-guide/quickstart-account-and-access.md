# Account and administrator-approved access

Helix access is granted by a platform administrator. Creating an account does not start a paid subscription or promise a free trial, credits, or unrestricted access.

## Create an account

Use the sign-up form with your name, email, and password. Then sign in to your workspace. The server, not a browser flag, decides which organization and features your session can access.

## Request access

Open **Access & Usage** and submit an access request for the features you need. Until an administrator approves it, the workspace shows **access pending admin approval**. A rejected, expired, or revoked grant does not authorize new searches. The current status is shown explicitly.

An approved grant determines feature availability and usage limits. It is not a purchasable plan. There is no checkout or payment-provider flow in the product.

## Start with Discover

After approval, use Discover to submit a competitor query. Progress and terminal status come from the server. Missing source information remains unknown; a successful login is not evidence that a data provider is configured or available.

Optional product areas may be hidden. Their presence in historical documentation does not mean they are enabled for your workspace.

## Account safety

Sign out when switching accounts or leaving a shared device. Session tokens remain in JavaScript-readable browser storage; do not treat a browser profile as a secure vault. The legal disclosure describes the current storage and deletion limitations.
